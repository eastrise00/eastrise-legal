Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

TierOne Defense ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use any of our mobile applications, including but not limited to CISSP Flash Cards, Security+ Flashcards, CEH Flash Cards, CISA Flash Cards, CISM Flash Cards, SSCP Flash Cards, Google Cybersecurity Flashcards, and any other educational apps published by TierOne Defense (collectively, "our Apps").

Information Collection

Our Apps are designed with your privacy in mind. We collect and store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Study progress and card mastery status
  • Quiz results and scores
  • Study session history
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and is not transmitted to any external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics sent to third parties

In-App Purchases

Our Apps offer optional subscription purchases to unlock premium content. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information on how Apple handles payment data.

Data Storage and Security

All your study data is stored locally on your device. If you delete an app, your local data for that app will be permanently removed. We recommend backing up your device regularly through iCloud or iTunes to preserve your progress.

Third-Party Services

Our Apps do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your study data stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our Apps do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our Apps are designed for students and professionals studying for professional certifications and career development.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. This policy applies to all current and future apps published by TierOne Defense.
