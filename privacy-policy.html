Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 19, 2026

CISSP Flash Cards ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information Collection

CISSP Flash Cards is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect and store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Study progress and card mastery status
  • Quiz results and scores
  • Study session history
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and is not transmitted to any external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics sent to third parties

In-App Purchases

Our app offers optional subscription purchases to unlock premium content. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for information on how Apple handles payment data.

Data Storage and Security

All your study data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, your local data will be permanently removed. We recommend backing up your device regularly through iCloud or iTunes to preserve your progress.

Third-Party Services

CISSP Flash Cards does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your study data stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for professionals studying for the CISSP certification.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
