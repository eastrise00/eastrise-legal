About Eastrise

Eastrise is an educational technology company focused on cybersecurity certification preparation. We build iOS apps that help professionals and students study for the certifications that matter most in the information security industry.

Each app in our suite is purpose-built for a specific certification, organized by official exam domains, and designed to make study time as efficient as possible. No ads, no distractions, no data collection — just the material you need to pass.

Whether you're starting your career with the Google Cybersecurity Certificate or pursuing advanced credentials like CISSP or CISM, we've built an app for your journey.

2025 Cyber Threat Landscape

The numbers behind why cybersecurity certifications matter more than ever

💰
$10.5 Trillion

Annual Cost of Cybercrime

Global cybercrime costs reached $10.5 trillion in 2025, projected to hit $15.63 trillion by 2029. That's $333,000 lost every minute — making cybercrime the world's third-largest economy.

🚨
44%

Breaches Involved Ransomware

Ransomware was involved in 44% of all breaches in 2025, up from 32% in 2024. Manufacturing saw a 61% surge in ransomware attacks, with the average incident costing $5.08 million.

🎣
80%

AI-Generated Phishing

80% of phishing attacks in 2025 were AI-generated, with 60% of recipients falling victim to GenAI-driven campaigns. Phishing remains the leading initial attack vector at 16% of all breaches.

🔗
2x

Supply Chain Attacks Doubled

Third-party and supply chain compromises accounted for 15% of breaches, doubling year-over-year. Major incidents hit Qantas (5.7M customers) and Salesloft (700+ organizations).

🏥
$10.22M

U.S. Breach Cost Leads World

The average data breach in the U.S. cost $10.22 million — more than double the global average. Healthcare remained the costliest industry at $7.42 million per breach for the 14th consecutive year.

👤
4.8 Million

Unfilled Cybersecurity Jobs

The global cybersecurity talent shortage grew to 4.8 million unfilled positions, a 19% gap increase. 66% of cyber professionals report significantly increased job stress.

Sources: IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2025, VikingCloud, Deepstrike, FBI IC3, WEF Global Cybersecurity Outlook

2026 Outlook

The threats shaping the cybersecurity landscape this year

87%

AI-Driven Vulnerabilities

87% of security leaders identified AI-related vulnerabilities as the fastest-growing cyber risk. AI-driven social engineering is the top predicted threat for 2026.

54%

Ransomware & Extortion

54% of organizations rank ransomware and extortion as their primary concern for 2026. Median ransom demands jumped from $12,738 to $59,556 in a single year.

35%

Insider Threats Rising

35% of leaders cite insider threats as a top concern. Shadow AI alone added $670K to average breach costs, and 63% of organizations still lack policies for internal AI use.
