What It Is

The cybersecurity news feed
built for security pros.

CyberThreats247 pulls breaking cyber threat intelligence from 19 trusted sources — Krebs, BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, Dark Reading, CISA, Mandiant, Microsoft Threat Intelligence, Cisco Talos, and more — into one unified feed. Every article is automatically tagged with threat categories and attributed APT groups so you can triage faster and stay ahead.

What You Get

Real-Time Threat Feed

Breaking cybersecurity news from 19+ sources, updated continuously. Threat level indicator, trending categories, and a hero carousel so the biggest stories never slip past you.

APT Attribution At A Glance

Every article is scanned for known nation-state and cybercriminal groups — Fancy Bear, Lazarus, APT28, Kimsuky, and dozens more — and auto-tagged so you can filter by adversary in one tap.

Smart Category Filters

Slice the feed by ransomware, data breach, zero-day, vulnerability, nation-state, supply chain, or AI threats. Your focus area, one tap away.

Full-Text On-Device Search

Instantly find any article across titles, summaries, and sources. All search runs on your device — your queries never leave the phone.

Bookmarks & Source Management

Save articles to read later. Show or hide individual sources to tailor the feed to the publishers you trust most.

Dark Mode, Always

Pure black OLED-friendly design with monospaced accents. Built for long reading sessions — and for the kind of people who live in a terminal.
