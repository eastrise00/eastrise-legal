Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Select CISSP Flash Cards
  • Tap Cancel Subscription

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you'd like refunded.

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes full access to Domain 1 (Security and Risk Management) and Domain 2 (Asset Security), with approximately 130 flashcards, plus unlimited quizzes and progress tracking for these domains.

What do I get with Premium?

Premium unlocks all 8 CISSP domains with 600+ flashcards, covering:

Is my study progress saved?

Yes! All your progress, including cards studied, mastery levels, quiz scores, and study streaks are saved locally on your device. We recommend regular device backups via iCloud to preserve your data.

The app isn't working correctly. What should I do?

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your device
  • Ensure you have the latest version from the App Store
  • If issues persist, contact us at cyberthreats247@gmail.com with details about the problem
